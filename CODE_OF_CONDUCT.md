# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the RenCal project a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and respect towards other people.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
public or private intimidation, publishing private information without
permission, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct. They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to
behaviour they consider unacceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to
[analytics@lowcarboncontracts.uk](mailto:analytics@lowcarboncontracts.uk).
Reports will be reviewed by the project maintainers and handled as
confidentially as practicable. Please do not use public issues for conduct
reports.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider the facts and context of each report and may take
actions including a private warning, temporary restriction, or permanent
restriction from project participation. Decisions should be proportionate to
the circumstances and documented for accountability.
