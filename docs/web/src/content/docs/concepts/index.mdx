# Core Concepts

Welcome to the Concepts section of the Low Carbon Contracts Company's documentation. This section is designed to provide you with a deep understanding of the fundamental principles and ideas that underpin our services and tools.

## What You'll Learn

In this section, you'll find detailed explanations and insights into the following key concepts:

- **Contract Management**: Understand how we manage and oversee contracts to ensure compliance and efficiency.
- **Energy Market**: Learn about the energy market dynamics and how our services interact with it.
- **Sustainability**: Discover our commitment to sustainability and how it influences our operations and services.
- **Technology Integration**: Explore the technologies we use and how they integrate with your systems.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Get to know the regulatory framework we operate within and how we ensure compliance.

## Getting Started

To get started, we recommend reading through each concept in the order presented. This will help you build a comprehensive understanding of our services and how they can benefit you.
